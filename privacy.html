Privacy

StyleDNA is a home style quiz from Josh Waters, REALTOR®, Dallas Collective Group, brokered by Agency Dallas Park Cities, LLC. Here's what it keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Your quiz answers and your StyleDNA result. That includes which home photos you look at and for how long, so the quiz can show you more of what you care about.
  • The name, email and phone you give us, and the areas, budget and home type you pick.
  • Your home board: the homes you save, your hearts, passes and comments.
  • When you came from StyleDNA or a board link, the homes you view and the searches you run on joshwaters.com.

Why

So Josh can send you homes that actually fit, tell you when a saved home changes price or status, and know what you liked before you talk.

Who sees it

Josh, and the CRM he uses to manage his clients. People on your home board see the homes, reactions and comments on that board, not your browsing. We don't sell your information.

Your choices

  • Turn off email alerts with the link in any alert email, and phone alerts from the board's Alerts button.
  • To have your information removed, email josh@dallascollectivegroup.com.
